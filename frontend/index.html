Help

Capture runs in OBS

OBSReplay uses its own OBS profile and scene collection. Save a replay with the app button or your global shortcut. Stopping capture discards the unsaved replay buffer.

ConnectionWaiting for OBSReplay.

Running in the tray

Hide the window to keep OBSReplay and its shortcuts running. Quitting OBSReplay leaves OBS and any active replay capture running, but the app’s shortcuts stop working.

What gets recorded

Game or display (Hybrid) records your display, including other windows and notifications, whenever no fullscreen game is captured. Fullscreen games only never records the desktop. Entire display always records the selected display. Microphone recording is opt-in.
